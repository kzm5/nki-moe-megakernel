- **Gate 1 met:** the validator catches m1 (bf16 accumulation; worst_pos), m2 (dropped renormalization; 4 rows) and m4 (wrong expert; router); the benign control m3 passes.
- **Gate 4 not met at the judged length (768): V is 4.47% faster than B** (threshold 5%; −0.470 ms, 2σ 0.072). At 2048 / 4096 / 8192 V clears 5% (6.67 / 9.22 / 9.52%), secondary results. B's library MoE is 1.437× faster than XLA at 768.
- **Where C's 0.285 ms over B comes from at 768** (one session, every step > 2σ): calling the megakernel's attention unfused from XLA costs +1.661 ms (B → E); hoisting its per-call mask/RoPE setup recovers −1.551 (E → Eh); fusion — the call boundary plus the vendored MoE kernel — is worth −0.395 (Eh → C); the collectives rewrite adds −0.185 (C → V).
- **Long context:** the gain that grows with context is the attention kernel, which an unfused kernel also gets: Eh − B goes +0.110 → −0.196 → −0.480 ms (768 / 2048 / 4096) while C − B goes −0.285 → −0.534 → −0.738. The fusion side (Eh − C) is +0.395 / +0.338 / +0.257 ms: it does not grow with context. V's lead over B keeps growing to 8192, more slowly; V is nearly flat in context (10.05 → 10.31 ms).
- **Accuracy:** every config passes v1.1. B is the most accurate (KL ratio vs XLA 0.871; median ρ 0.90–0.91; router flips 8.11% vs XLA 9.52%); E 0.967, C 0.961, V 0.960. The collectives change does not move accuracy: C and V are bit-identical on 62/64 prompts at 768, all prompts at 2048, and 16/16 long-context prompts.
- **Long-context validation:** the 2048 and 4096 builds of XLA, B, C, V pass v1.1 at positions < 768; a supplementary corpus (16 prompts of 1,400–1,895 tokens, frozen separately) passes at 2048 for all four (supplementary, not the v1 result).
- **V is deterministic:** 5 separate-process captures bit-identical; the merged version (with trace-time asserts) is bit-identical to them.
- **C hangs at runtime at 8192** (one attempt; device wedged until a driver reload). V, with the same attention kernel, runs (Hypothesis: C's per-layer SB2SB all-reduce at that bucket).

## Precision studies (CPU-golden emulations)
- H2.2: bf16 expert-GEMM inputs raise mean KL vs golden from ~0 to 6.85e-4; bf16 accumulation to 9.87e-4.
- H2.4: a bf16-per-add TP all-reduce gives +43% mean KL vs an fp32 sum (9.25e-4 vs 6.49e-4). On the device C (bf16 all-reduce) and V (fp32 sum) are nearly identical (above) — Hypothesis: trn2's bf16 all-reduce does not round per add, so all-reduce precision is not a lever here.
- H2.6: NKI builds start ~30% more accurate than XLA at layer 0 and converge by layer 47; all builds peak at layers 33–35.

## Agent study (Q3 T4; setup A = compile + verdict + p50, setup C = + validator rows + profile; 6 h each)
All five runs (A1, A2, C1, C2 partial, C2r) beat C within budget; best eligible 10.023–10.101 ms (harness n=2), within harness noise of each other.
