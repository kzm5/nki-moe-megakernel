   (and fp32 top-k), and score it against golden with the validator's rows.
   **Decision rule:** if the emulated flip rate falls by less than 2 pp, or the mean KL by less than 10%, against
   the bf16-router emulation, skip step 2 and report the emulation as the result.
2. **Kernel (time-box 12 h).** Library MoE config (B): compute router logits and top-k in fp32. Use whichever is
   simpler: an fp32 router inside a copied, env-gated MoE TKG kernel, or an fp32 router in XLA feeding the kernel,
   if the kernel accepts precomputed routing. Validator v1.1, then latency vs B. Report flip rate, ρ, KL, perplexity
   and Δlatency: one Pareto point. Do the megakernel (C) too only if B takes under 6 h.
