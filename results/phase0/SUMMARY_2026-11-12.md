## Top 3 findings

1. **On trn2 the megakernel's lead is an attention effect, not fusion.** Profile: no sync-engine or DMA-packet saving (trn3 had −64% sync, −43% packets); the megakernel spends +9 µs/layer more in collectives; library MoE's XLA attention adds +10–12 µs/layer from the smallest to the largest bucket while the megakernel stays flat. NxDI's own NKI attention kernel (config D) also removes the context slope (12.08 ms at both 768 and 2048) but costs +1.75 ms/step on trn2, so it can't serve as a neutral stand-in to isolate fusion. (Measured; identity of the growing XLA ops Hypothesis.)
2. **Both NKI builds are as accurate as XLA against fp32, except for occasional gross single-step errors.** Before divergence, ρ = 0.96 / 1.05 (inside the 1.25 gate). But ~40–50% of first divergences are not near-ties: the NKI output at an identical context is wrong by up to 25 nats, golden sides with XLA every time, and three such positions are shared by both builds. The day-1 hypothesis that XLA was the outlier is refuted (at p01@15 golden says " is" at 33.6; both NKI builds say " United", golden rank 298). (Measured; shared-MoE-kernel cause Hypothesis.)
3. **The fusion gain keeps growing with context but slowly:** 1.015–1.021× at 768, 1.059× at 2048, 1.072× at 4096. The megakernel is no longer flat at 4096 (+0.21 ms), consistent with its attention reading the full `seq_len` KV cache every step. Between-day drift (~0.04 ms) exceeds within-day std, so small config differences need same-session runs. (Measured.)

## What failed

- **Profiling attempts 1–2:** `Runtime.profile_enable` doesn't cover NxDI's execution path (no files); session-mode inspect gave NTFFs without NEFFs. Fixed with in-process `nrt_inspect_begin/stop` in `model` mode plus `SPMDModel.save_neff`.
- **Config D attempt 1:** `QKV kernel only supported when fused_qkv is TRUE`; needed `--fused-qkv`.
- **Golden v1 router capture:** saved router top-9 for position 0 only (output values unaffected); re-ran (v2). CPU fp32 is not bit-reproducible across thread schedules (drift rel-L2 ≤ 3e-4).
- **Not run:** Task 5 bisect (trigger ρ > 1.25 not met as specified; see finding 2). Noise floor reduced to 5 runs per config (timing run 14:15 > 8 min).

## Recommended next 3 experiments

1. **Bisect the gross NKI errors against golden** (Task 5 on p01@15, p06@4, p07@45): per-layer captures of the NKI builds at those positions vs golden's per-layer hidden states and router top-9 (`~/runs/golden_v2`), to find the first layer and op that departs. The shared positions point at the NKI MoE TKG kernel family.
2. **Add an at-divergence term to the correctness gate**, e.g. max per-position KL(golden‖build) over all teacher-forced positions, run on a larger corpus: the pre-divergence ρ passes builds that make 25-nat single-step errors.
3. **Profile config D** (one ~25 min capture) to locate its +36 µs/layer and decide whether a tuned NKI attention kernel without fusion recovers the megakernel's whole trn2 advantage — which would make Phase 4's fusion-depth sweep a likely negative result on trn2.
