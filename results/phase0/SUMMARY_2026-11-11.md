## Top 3 findings

1. **On trn2 the library MoE kernel captures almost all of the decode gain** (1.45× over XLA). The megakernel's fusion adds a real but small 2% at 768 tokens (vs 1.16× on trn3 in the README). The earlier 2.44× E2E gap was an artifact: the megakernel forces on-device sampling while XLA configs sampled on the host. (Measured)
2. **The megakernel's advantage grows with context.** Its decode latency is flat (10.1–10.2 ms from 256 to 2048) while library MoE grows 6%, giving 1.06× p50 / 1.08× p99 at 2048. Hypothesis: the fused attention hides context-proportional KV traffic that the XLA attention path pays per layer. (Measured trend; mechanism Hypothesis.)
3. **Neither NKI build passes NxDI logit_validation against XLA**, at the repo tol_map or rtol 1e-2 (0/20 each). Both are numerically alike (pre-divergence KL ≈ 2e-3 nats); most divergences are bf16 near-ties, but logit errors reach 3.5–7.7% of max |logit| even where argmax never diverges. Two divergences pick the same off-ranked token in both NKI builds — the XLA reference may be the outlier there. Also: `--top-k 1` alone is not greedy (8.9% non-argmax tokens); `--do-sample ""` is required. (Measured; near-tie reading Hypothesis.)

## What failed

- **Per-layer profile (Task 4):** runtime 2.32 writes NTFF v7; installed `aws-neuronx-tools` 2.28 reads only v1–6 (`neuron-profile` and `neuron-explorer` both refuse). Runtime-inspect also profiled every NEFF (26 GB) and filled the disk. No per-layer table; the trn2-vs-trn3 explanation remains Hypothesis.
- **First XLA greedy build:** non-greedy because main.py's `--do-sample` defaults True (superseded, rebuilt).
- **Setup (earlier):** SDK 2.30's bundled nkilib breaks the legacy MoE kernels; seq 640 fails the LNC=2 `seq_len % 256` check; hardcoded `trn3` targets blocked every trn2 megakernel load (fixed).

## Recommended next 3 experiments

1. **Upgrade `aws-neuronx-tools` to the 2.30 line (`2.30.10.0`) and redo Task 4**, capturing only the TKG NEFF, at seq 768 and 2048 — to measure where the megakernel's context-dependent gain comes from (attention KV DMA, AllReduce, SBUF spill) and why trn2 fusion gains are ~8× smaller than trn3's.
2. **Make validation decisive:** (a) XLA-vs-XLA run-to-run baseline to bound achievable tolerance; (b) teacher-forced per-position comparison (feed reference tokens) to remove divergence cascades; (c) inspect router top-8 logits at p01@15 / p06@4 to test the "XLA reference is the outlier" hypothesis.
3. **Extend the sweep to 4096/8192 with n=3** for both configs (pre-sharded to cut load time and RAM peaks), to see whether the megakernel stays flat and the gain keeps growing toward the long-context regime; also fix the megakernel's +19.5 ms CTE regression (its model class's CTE compile flags).
