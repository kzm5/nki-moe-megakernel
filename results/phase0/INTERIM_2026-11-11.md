At the smallest bucket (128) both are ~193 µs/layer.

**Which explanation the data supports:** *attention scaling with context.* Library MoE grows +10–12 µs/layer from bucket 128 to the largest bucket; the megakernel stays flat (+0.1–0.3). The graph-time gap (0.51 ms at 768, 0.59 ms at 2048) matches the measured benchmark gap at 2048 (0.61 ms). The growth sits in untagged XLA ops whose extra HBM reads equal the extra K/V bytes (Measured; that these are XLA's QK/softmax/PV is Hypothesis).
Not supported: *SBUF spill* (megakernel spills 19–24 KB per graph, less than lib MoE's 213 KB) and *boundary cost* (sync engine 58.5 vs 60.1 µs, same DMA packet count/size — none of trn3's savings). *Collectives* are an offsetting cost: megakernel CC +9 µs/layer (trn3: +14).
So on trn2 the megakernel's lead is an attention-kernel effect, not fusion (Task 4 config D tests this directly).

## 2. fp32 golden reference

`tools/golden_fp32.py`: layerwise HF decoder layers, bf16 mode **bit-identical** to stock HF; fp32 over 20 prompts × (prompt + 128 tokens) in 106 s, 4.4 GiB peak.

| vs fp32 golden, positions before first divergence | XLA | Library MoE | Megakernel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positions | 2,560 | 936 | 711 |
| rel-L2 mean | 0.0417 | 0.0418 | 0.0460 |
| KL(golden‖build) mean | 1.9e-3 | 2.2e-3 | 2.2e-3 |
| top-1 = golden | 98.6% | 98.9% | 99.0% |
| **ρ = err(build)/err(XLA)** | 1 | **0.96** | **1.05** |

At the first-divergence positions (context still identical to XLA), the divergences split cleanly:
- **Benign** (12 lib MoE, 9 megakernel): bf16 near-ties; golden sides with the NKI build about half the time.
- **Gross** (7 of 19 lib MoE, 9 of 18 megakernel): KL(golden‖build) 0.9–25 nats vs ≤ 0.01 for XLA (ρ at the position 8–63); golden sides with XLA every time.

The two shared "unlikely token" spots: golden agrees with XLA. p01@15, after "…The capital of Spain": golden " is" (33.6), both NKI builds " United" (golden rank 298). p06@4, after "I have a new": golden " pair", both NKI builds " of" (rank 880). **The XLA-outlier hypothesis is refuted** (Measured).

**Decision needed:** both builds pass the ρ ≤ 1.25 trigger as specified (pre-divergence), so Task 5 (bisect) is skipped per the queue. But the gross single-step errors are invisible to that metric by construction, are shared by both NKI paths, and look like a real defect (Hypothesis: the shared NKI MoE TKG kernel family). I recommend running Task 5 on p01@15 and p06@4 anyway; say if you want that.
